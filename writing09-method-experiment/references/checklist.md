# Writing 09 extraction checklist

- Steps: ordered inputs, operations, outputs, and decision conditions.
- Settings: hardware, software, versions, runtime, preprocessing, and evaluation protocol.
- Parameters: value, unit, target, source, and status.
- Data: name, version, acquisition, preprocessing, splits, and exclusions.
- Controls: baseline, ablation, comparison, and repetitions.
- Reproduction: repository revision, environment, command, seed, and data access.
- Gaps: missing, conflicting, or author-confirmation items with evidence.

Questions in this file are a checklist, not evidence. Examples are illustrative only.
